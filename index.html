About

The artist
behind the look

I believe makeup is more than technique — it's the art of seeing someone clearly and drawing out what's already luminous in them. Every face tells a different story, and my work is to tell it beautifully.

Based in Bengaluru, I work with brides, photographers, brands, and individuals who value artistry that is thoughtful, precise, and deeply personal. My work spans bridal, editorial, film, and everyday transformations.

Whether it's the quiet intimacy of a bridal morning or the bold energy of an editorial shoot, I bring the same focus to every sitting: presence, skill, and care.
